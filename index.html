The drawing follows container GPLU 210521 2 through the nine stations. A free-days clock starts when the container is discharged at Alexandria; in this illustration it is cleared, delivered and returned empty inside five free days. Free days vary by shipping line and port.

  1. Station01

    Quote & booking

    One teamFrom quote to your door

    Tell us the cargo, the route and the ready date. We send a quote with every charge on its own line, then book space and equipment with the shipping line.

    What GPL does

    • Quote our fees, line and port charges, and duties on separate lines
    • Book space and equipment with the shipping line
    • List the documents your shipment will need

  2. Station02

    ACI pre-registration on Nafeza

    48hBefore the cargo leaves origin

    Before loading, the shipment is registered on Nafeza, Egypt’s trade single window, and gets a 19-digit ACID. Your supplier uploads the documents on CargoX. No ACID, no clearance.

    What GPL does

    • Check your Nafeza registration and the ACID request
    • Help your supplier upload the invoice, B/L, packing list and certificate of origin on CargoX
    • Make sure the ACID is on every document, including the B/L

  3. Station03

    Pickup at origin

    SealedContainer and seal numbers recorded

    The cargo is stuffed at your supplier’s door, the container is sealed and trucked to the port of loading.

    What GPL does

    • Coordinate pickup and stuffing with your supplier
    • Record the container and seal numbers
    • Send you a pre-alert with the booking details

  4. Station04

    Port of loading

    B/LIssued with your ACID on it

    The container is gated in and loaded on board. The bill of lading is issued, and it must carry the ACID, or the goods cannot be cleared in Egypt.

    What GPL does

    • Check the draft B/L against the ACID before it is issued
    • Confirm the vessel, the loading and the ETA
    • Send you the B/L copy

  5. Station05

    At sea

    0 daysOf free time used at sea

    While the ship sails, the paperwork gets settled. Names, HS codes, values and weights must match across the ACID, invoice, B/L and certificate of origin.

    What GPL does

    • Check every document against the ACID
    • Agree the HS codes with you and confirm any permits
    • Tell you the duties and import rules before arrival

  6. Station06

    Arrival at Alexandria

    Day 0Free time starts at discharge

    The vessel discharges at Alexandria or Dekheila, or at Sokhna, Port Said or Damietta, and the free-time clock starts. Since June 2026, clearance can start on arrival without waiting for the line’s delivery order.

    What GPL does

    • Start clearance the day the ship arrives
    • Collect the delivery order in parallel
    • Count your free days from discharge

  7. Station07

    Declaration & inspection

    FiledElectronic customs declaration

    We file the customs declaration electronically. Customs examines the cargo where required, along with any agency checks your cargo needs.

    What GPL does

    • File the declaration with the agreed HS codes
    • Attend the examination at the terminal
    • Handle any agency checks for your cargo

  8. Station08

    Duties paid & release

    Line by lineEvery amount shown before it is paid

    We pay duties and VAT on your behalf, or you pay directly. Either way you see every amount before it is paid. Then the container is released.

    What GPL does

    • Show you duties and VAT before payment
    • Pay on your behalf, or guide your own payment
    • Collect the release and book the truck

  9. Station09

    Truck to your door

    DeliveredEmpty back to the depot inside free time

    We truck the container to your factory or warehouse, or to ours, and return the empty to the line’s depot before free time runs out.

    What GPL does

    • Truck the container to your door or our warehouse
    • Return the empty to the line’s depot
    • Send one invoice for the whole move

Why importers and exporters choose GPL.

Four commitments

  1. W/01

    No demurrage surprises.

    Free time on Egypt imports is often only 5–6 days. We count it from the day the vessel discharges and plan clearance and trucking to finish inside it. If a delay puts them at risk, you hear it from us first, while there is still time to act.

    Invoices you can read.
