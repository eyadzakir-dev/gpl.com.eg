  1. R/01 · One point

    The point where it all connects.

    Egypt sits where the Mediterranean, the Suez Canal and the Red Sea meet. From our office in Alexandria we run freight and customs clearance through all of the country’s main gateways.

  2. R/02 · Four gateways

    Five ports on two seas.

    Alexandria and Dekheila, Damietta and Port Said face the Mediterranean. Sokhna faces the Red Sea and is the nearest port to Greater Cairo.

  3. R/03 · West

    Across the Med to Europe. Over the Atlantic to the US.

    Short sea lanes run to Piraeus, Mersin, Genoa and Valencia, and on through Gibraltar to Rotterdam and Hamburg. Atlantic lanes reach New York and Savannah on the US East Coast.

    EuropeNorth America

  4. R/04 · East

    Through Suez to Asia. Down the Red Sea to the Gulf.

    Port Said opens onto the Suez Canal and the lanes to Nhava Sheva, Shanghai and Ningbo. Sokhna faces the Red Sea, with lanes to Jeddah and Jebel Ali.

    Red Sea and Suez routings can change voyage by voyage. We confirm the routing and transit with every booking.

  5. R/05 · Trade lanes

    Europe, North America, Asia and Africa.

    Freight to and from every region we serve, with customs clearance in Egypt run by the same team.

    EuropeNorth AmericaAsiaAfricaMiddle East

    Lanes shown are the main trade routes from Egypt’s ports, not a sailing schedule.

  6. R/06 · Green Point

    Every lane, one point of contact.

    Book the freight, clear customs, truck the container and store the goods with one team. You get one contact and one invoice for the whole move.

02

Five ports, one team.

Clearance · freight · trucking

Whichever gateway suits your cargo, the same team books the freight, clears customs and trucks the container.

  • EGALY

    Alexandria

    Mediterranean

    Egypt’s busiest gateway: its port authority, which also runs Dekheila, handles about 60% of the country’s foreign trade. Close to our head office in El Manshya.

  • EGEDK

    Dekheila

    Mediterranean

    Alexandria’s second harbour, west of the city, run by the same port authority.

  • EGDAM

    Damietta

    Mediterranean

    Container port for the Nile Delta, with the new DACT terminal open since February 2026.

  • EGPSD

    Port Said

    Mediterranean · Suez Canal

    East Port Said is a transshipment hub; West Port Said handles containers, general cargo and RORO.

  • EGSOK

    Sokhna

    Red Sea

    Egypt’s main gateway to the Gulf, East Africa and Asia, and the nearest port to Greater Cairo.

Red Sea and Suez routings can change voyage by voyage. We confirm the routing and transit with every booking.
