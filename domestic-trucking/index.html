What's included.

Standard scope

  • Full truckload (FTL)

    The whole truck carries only your goods, straight from pickup to drop.

07

Questions about trucking.

All FAQs →

What is the difference between FTL and LTL?

FTL means the whole truck carries only your goods. LTL means your goods share the truck with other shipments and you pay for the space you use.

Can you pick up straight from the port?

Yes. Port-to-warehouse trucking is one of our core runs, and we time it with customs release.

Can I change the delivery date?

Usually yes. Tell us as early as possible and we will re-plan the run.

Is domestic trucking the same as inland transportation?

No. Inland transportation moves shipping containers between the port and your site. Domestic trucking moves your goods, by full or part truckload, between locations inside Egypt.
