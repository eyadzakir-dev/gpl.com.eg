01

Built inside a manufacturing group.

Our story

Green Point Logistics was founded in Alexandria in 2021 as the logistics arm of Three Stars Group, a garment manufacturer and exporter established in the city in 2008.

A manufacturer lives by its delivery dates. Raw materials have to clear on time, finished orders have to sail on time, and a container held at the port costs money every day. GPL was set up to control all of that in-house: freight, customs clearance, trucking and storage, run by one team.

We grew quickly. A second branch opened in 2022. We built partnerships with international airlines and shipping lines and a network of agents abroad. Consolidation and LCL became a particular strength.

Today we offer the same service to importers and exporters outside the group: food and agri exporters, chemical and engineering importers, furniture makers and garment factories that want a partner who understands deadlines.

05

Part of Three Stars Group.

Group member 04 · GPL

Three Stars Group has four member companies: Three Stars Fashion, Three Stars Apparels, Golden Stars Textile and Green Point Logistics, the group’s logistics arm.

The group was established in Alexandria in 2008 and makes knitted and woven garments for international brands and retailers. GPL runs the group’s logistics: order consolidation, export documents, customs clearance, freight and delivery.

What it means for you

  • We ship like a manufacturer.

    We know what a missed sailing does to a production plan, because we have lived it.

  • Garment and QIZ know-how.

    Retailer packing, consolidation, QIZ and Euro 1 paperwork are daily work for us, not special requests.

  • Stable and accountable.

    You deal with a company that is part of an established Alexandria group, with a name to protect.
