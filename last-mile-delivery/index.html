07

Questions about last-mile delivery.

All FAQs →

Do you deliver to consumers?

Our last-mile service is built for businesses delivering to their customers, shops and sites. Ask us about your case.

Can goods go straight from the port to my customer?

Yes, for full loads we can truck directly from the port. Smaller drops go through our warehouse first.

Do I get proof of delivery?

Yes, every drop is signed for and the proof is sent to you.
