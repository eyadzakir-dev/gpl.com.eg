04

How an air shipment runs.

5 steps

  1. 01

    Quote

    You send dimensions, weight and ready date; we quote on chargeable weight.

  2. 02

    Booking

    We book the flight and arrange pickup.

  3. 03

    Documents

    Air waybill, invoice and packing list checked. For imports, the ACID is registered and your supplier uploads the documents at least 8 hours before take-off.

  4. 04

    Flight and arrival

    We track the flight and prepare the clearance file before landing.

  5. 05

    Clearance and delivery

    Customs release at the airport, then delivery to your door.

05

Documents we need.

ACI · air imports

Documents go in 8 hours before take-off.

Since 1 January 2026, air imports into Egypt need an ACID number, and your supplier must upload the documents at least 8 hours before the flight leaves. We set it up with you when we book.

07

Air freight questions.

All FAQs →

How is air freight priced?

By chargeable weight: the higher of the actual weight and the volume weight. Bulky, light cargo is charged on its size. Send us the dimensions of each piece and we will calculate it.

Is air freight worth it over sea?

For urgent, small or high-value cargo, often yes: you save days or weeks and hold less stock. For heavy or bulky cargo, sea is usually far cheaper. We can quote both.

Can you clear my air shipment on arrival?

Yes. We prepare the clearance file before the flight lands, so the cargo can be released as soon as customs allows.

Does ACI apply to air cargo?

Yes. Since 1 January 2026, air imports into Egypt need an ACID number, and your supplier must upload the documents at least 8 hours before take-off. We set it up with you when we book.
