01

The last leg decides the bill.

Port-to-door haulage

A cleared container that waits at the port still costs money, and a late empty return adds detention charges. Because we clear and truck the same container, the truck is booked for the day of release, not the day after.

We move full containers from the port to your site and bring the empty back to the shipping line's depot, and we run export containers the other way.

Moving pallets or part loads rather than a container? Domestic trucking carries your goods by full or part truckload between locations inside Egypt, while inland transportation hauls the shipping container itself.

What's included.

04

How a haulage move runs.

Import · 4 steps

  1. 01

    Plan

    We confirm the delivery address, opening hours and the free days left.

  2. 02

    Release and pickup

    On customs release, the truck collects the container at the terminal.

  3. 03

    Delivery

    Delivered to your site; you unload within the agreed time.

  4. 04

    Empty return

    We return the empty to the line's depot and send you the return receipt.

Exporting? The run reverses: we collect the empty from the depot, you load it at your site, and we deliver the full container to the terminal in time for the vessel.

07

Questions about haulage.

All FAQs →

What is the difference between inland transportation and domestic trucking?

Inland transportation moves shipping containers between the port and your site. Domestic trucking moves your goods, by full or part truckload, between locations inside Egypt.

Who returns the empty container?

We do. Returning it on time avoids detention charges from the shipping line, so we plan the return when we plan the delivery.

Can you deliver to a free zone?

Yes. We move containers between the ports and Egypt's free zones, together with the customs paperwork.

Do you also clear the container?

Yes. When we clear and truck the same container, the truck is booked for release day. If another broker clears it, we collect as soon as you send us the release.
