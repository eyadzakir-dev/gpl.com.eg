01

Clearance is where time and money are lost.

Fast · predictable · transparent

In 2024 the average container at Alexandria took 8.6 days from vessel arrival to gate-out, and only about 2.8 of those days were customs and inspection. The rest is paperwork, payments and waiting, while free time is often only 5–6 days. A missing ACID number, a wrong HS code or a late permit can hold a container for days while demurrage and storage charges run.

We prevent that by working before the ship arrives: ACI registration on Nafeza, document checks, HS classification and any agency approvals. When the cargo lands, clearance starts the same day. You see the duties, taxes and charges before they are paid.

What a late box costs

About USD 2,500 for one 40′ container.

Example under one major carrier's 2026 tariff: a 40′ container that leaves Alexandria port on day 20 and returns empty on day 22 runs up about USD 2,500 in demurrage, detention and storage. Cleared and returned inside free time, the same box costs nothing extra.

04

How clearance runs.

Sea import · 8 steps

Free days start when the container is discharged, not when clearance starts. So the work that decides your bill happens before the ship arrives.

  1. 01

    You + supplier + us · before loading

    ACI on Nafeza

    At least 48 hours before the cargo leaves the export country, the shipment is registered on Nafeza and gets a 19-digit ACID. Your supplier uploads the invoice, bill of lading, packing list and certificate of origin on CargoX. No ACID, no clearance.

  2. 02

    Shipping line · day 0

    Arrival and manifest

    The vessel arrives, the line's agent lodges the manifest and the container is discharged. The free-days clock starts. Since June 2026 clearance can start on arrival, without waiting for the line's delivery order, so we start the same day.

  3. 03

    Us

    Customs declaration

    We file the declaration electronically with the ACID, the agreed HS codes, values and weights, and collect the delivery order from the line in parallel.

  4. 04

    Customs + us

    Document review

    Names, HS codes, values and weights must match across the ACID, invoice, bill of lading and certificate of origin. Because we checked them before arrival, questions are answered on the spot.

  5. 05

    Customs + agencies

    Inspection

    Depending on the goods and customs' risk assessment, the cargo is examined, and some products need checks by other authorities. We attend for you and tell you in advance what to expect.

  6. 06

    You or us

    Duties and VAT

    Duties, VAT and any other taxes are assessed and paid before release, by us on your behalf or by you directly. Either way you see every amount before it is paid.

  7. 07

    Customs

    Release

    Customs issues the release. With the line's delivery order in hand, the container is free to leave the terminal.

  8. 08

    Us · release day

    Truck out

    The truck we booked for release day collects the container, delivers it to your site and returns the empty to the line's depot before free time runs out.

Steps and timing vary by cargo, port and agency. ACI also applies to air imports since 1 January 2026, with documents due 8 hours before take-off. We tell you up front which steps apply to your shipment.

Demurrage

Free days run from discharge. Every day after them is billed.

Published import free time for dry containers in Egypt is typically 5–6 days, and 3–4 days for reefers, counted from discharge until the empty is returned. After that the shipping line bills demurrage and detention per container per day, about USD 45–125 for a 20′ and USD 65–185 for a 40′ under 2026 tariffs, and the terminal adds storage. We plan clearance inside the window and warn you the moment anything threatens it.

Export clearance, in five steps.

Sea export · since July 2026

  1. 01 You + us

    UCR on Nafeza

    Every sea export needs a 19-digit UCR before booking. A booking without one is rejected, and the UCR and shipper cannot be changed later.

  2. 02 Us

    Booking and documents

    We book the sailing or flight and check the invoice, packing list and the certificate of origin your buyer's market needs.

  3. 03 Us + customs

    Export declaration

    We file the export declaration. Some goods, such as food and agri products, are inspected before export.

  4. 04 Line

    Loading

    The cargo is loaded and the line or airline issues the bill of lading or air waybill.

  5. 05 Us

    Rebate file

    You get the customs export certificate, bill of lading and certificate of origin for your export-rebate claim.

06

Free-zone procedures.

Public and private free zones

Goods for a company licensed in an Egyptian free zone follow their own route through customs. We handle every move, from the port into the zone, out to export, and into the local market.

  1. Port → zone

    Into the zone

    Goods from abroad for a free-zone company enter without paying customs duties or VAT, as long as they stay in the zone or are re-exported. We clear them at the port and truck them to the zone with the customs paperwork.

  2. Zone → abroad

    Out to export

    Finished goods leave the zone for export with the export documents and the certificate of origin your buyer needs.

  3. Zone → Egypt

    Into the local market

    Goods released from a zone into Egypt are cleared like an import, and duties and taxes become due on entry. We estimate them before the move.

  4. Zone ↔ zone

    Between zones

    Transfers between free zones, with the paperwork for both sides and the haulage in between.

07

QIZ and certificates of origin for garment exporters.

From inside Three Stars Group

We are the logistics arm of Three Stars Group, a garment manufacturer in Alexandria since 2008. We ship the group's own exports, so QIZ paperwork, EUR.1 and retailer requirements are daily work for us, not special requests.

QIZ (Qualifying Industrial Zones) lets goods made by qualifying factories in designated Egyptian zones enter the United States free of normal US import duty, if they contain at least 35% qualifying content, including 10.5% Israeli input. Garments are the main QIZ product.

United States, 2026

QIZ is no longer duty-free in full.

Since 24 July 2026, Egyptian goods pay an additional 12.5% US duty under Section 301, and no QIZ exemption has been announced. Include it in your landed cost.

Ask us about QIZ consulting when you request a quote.

09

Questions about clearance.

All FAQs →

What is ACI and do I need it?

Yes, for every sea import (since October 2021) and every air import (since 1 January 2026). The shipment is registered on Nafeza at least 48 hours before it leaves the export country, and the importer and exporter receive a 19-digit ACID number. Your supplier uploads the documents on CargoX, and the ACID must appear on every document, including the bill of lading or air waybill. Without it the goods are not cleared. We guide you and your supplier through each step.

How do you help me avoid demurrage?

We prepare the clearance before the vessel arrives: ACI registration, document checks, HS codes and any permits. We count your free days from discharge and book the truck for the day of release. Since June 2026 clearance can start on arrival, without waiting for the line's delivery order, and we use that. If something threatens your free days, such as a missing document or an inspection, we tell you at once and agree the fastest fix with you.

How are duties and VAT calculated?

Duties depend on the HS code and origin of the goods, applied to the customs value. VAT and any other taxes are added on top. We estimate the total before arrival so there are no surprises.

Will my cargo be inspected?

It depends on the goods and the customs risk assessment. Some products also need checks by other agencies, such as food safety or import control. We tell you in advance what to expect and attend the inspection for you.

Do I need a UCR number to export?

Yes, for sea exports. Since July 2026 every sea export booking needs a 19-digit UCR from Nafeza, and shipping lines reject bookings without one. The UCR and the shipper cannot be changed afterwards, so we set it up correctly before the booking is made.

Can you clear goods into a free zone?

Yes. Goods for a company in a free zone enter without import duties; duties are paid only if they later enter the Egyptian market. We handle both moves.
