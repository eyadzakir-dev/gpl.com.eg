04

How import clearance works.

Sea import · Egypt · 5 stamps

In 2024 the average container at Alexandria took 8.6 days to leave the port. Published free time is often only 5–6 days. The gap is closed before the ship arrives: registration, documents and HS codes are settled while your cargo is still at sea.

Source: Egyptian Customs Authority, Time Release Study 2024; carrier tariffs, 2026.

Scroll · five stamps, one file ↓

  1. ACI record · NafezaSample

    ACID 0000 0000 0000 0000 000

    ACIRegisteredNafeza · CargoX

    Stamp 01 ACI registered

    Pre-register on Nafeza.

    At least 48 hours before your cargo leaves the export country, the shipment is registered on Nafeza, Egypt’s trade single window, and gets a 19-digit ACID number. Your supplier uploads the invoice, bill of lading, packing list and certificate of origin on CargoX, and the ACID goes on every document. No ACID, no clearance.

    GPL does We register you, onboard your supplier on CargoX and chase the uploads.

    Arrival, declaration and inspection.

    The vessel discharges and the free-time clock starts. Since June 2026, clearance can begin on arrival without waiting for the shipping line’s delivery order, so we file the customs declaration electronically the same day and collect the delivery order in parallel. Customs examines the cargo where required, along with any agency checks.

    GPL does We tell you in advance what to expect and attend the inspection for you.

Why importers and exporters choose GPL.

Four commitments

Reverse of Form GPL-00Four commitments · plain English

  1. Clause W/01

    No demurrage surprises.

    Free time on Egypt imports is often only 5–6 days. We count it from the day the vessel discharges and plan clearance and trucking to finish inside it. If a delay puts them at risk, you hear it from us first, while there is still time to act.

    Free days · tracked

  2. Clause W/02

    Invoices you can read.

    Our fees, shipping-line and port charges, and customs duties and taxes sit on separate lines. Government charges are passed on at cost, with receipts on request.

    Line by line

    One partner, door to door.

    Sea, air, trucking, warehousing and customs clearance under one roof. One contact follows your shipment from booking to delivery.
