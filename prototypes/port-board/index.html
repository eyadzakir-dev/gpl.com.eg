How import clearance works.

Sea import · Egypt · 6 steps

In 2024 the average container at Alexandria took 8.6 days to leave the port. Published free time is often only 5–6 days. The gap is closed before the ship arrives: registration, documents and HS codes are settled while your cargo is still at sea.

Illustration of a sea import planned inside five free days; free days vary by shipping line and port. Before the ship arrives, the shipment is pre-registered on Nafeza and the documents and HS codes are checked. The free-time clock starts at discharge. Clearance starts on arrival, then the declaration with duties and VAT, inspection and release, gate-out and delivery, and the empty container goes back on day five, before free time ends. For comparison, the 2024 average at Alexandria was 8.6 days from arrival to gate-out, well past the free time. After free time, a 40-foot container costs USD 65 to 80 a day, rising to USD 155 to 185.

  1. 01

    Pre-register on Nafeza (ACI).

    At least 48 hours before your cargo leaves the export country, the shipment is registered on Nafeza, Egypt’s trade single window, and gets a 19-digit ACID number. Your supplier uploads the invoice, bill of lading, packing list and certificate of origin on CargoX. No ACID, no clearance.

  2. 02

    Check documents and HS codes.

    Names, HS codes, values and weights must match across the ACID, invoice, bill of lading and certificate of origin. We check them and agree the HS codes with you, so duties are known before arrival.

  3. 03

    Arrival: clearance starts.

    The vessel discharges and the free-time clock starts. Since June 2026, clearance can begin on arrival without waiting for the shipping line’s delivery order, so we start the same day.

  4. 04

    Customs declaration.

    We file the declaration electronically and pay duties and VAT on your behalf, or you pay directly. Either way you see every amount before it is paid.

  5. 05

    Inspection and release.

    Customs examines the cargo where required, along with any agency checks. Once cleared, the container is released.

  6. 06

    Gate-out and delivery.

    We truck the container to your door and return the empty to the line’s depot before free time runs out.

Steps and timing vary by cargo type, port and agency. ACI also applies to air imports since 1 January 2026. We tell you up front which steps apply to your shipment.
