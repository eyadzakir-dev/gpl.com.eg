Green Point Logistics: homepage prototypes

Five homepage directions built in the shared "Production Line" design system in GPL green. The final homepage combines the strongest parts of each.

  • The Clearance Line

    One container, nine stations. A scroll-pinned line follows a container from booking through ACI, customs and release to the customer's door.

  • Tile Sphere

    Three.js hero built from the logo: diamond tiles assemble into the GPL sphere, then become a globe of trade lanes from Egypt's ports.

  • Port Board

    Split-flap port board, an interactive map of Egypt's five ports, and a free-days meter that explains demurrage.

  • Manifest

    The page as a shipment's document set: a bill-of-lading hero and customs stamps that press on as you scroll.

  • Shipment Brief

    Conversion first: build a shipment brief in the hero, see the typical plan, and send it as a prefilled quote request.
