ALX

Alexandria

Mediterranean · EGALY

Egypt’s busiest gateway. Its port authority, which also runs Dekheila, handles about 60% of the country’s foreign trade.

Terminal
Hutchison Ports AICT, running container terminals here since 2005
Serves
Alexandria QIZ and Borg El Arab, one of Egypt’s main garment-factory areas
Main lanes
Europe and the Mediterranean · US East Coast via Gibraltar

PSD

Port Said

Mediterranean · Suez Canal · EGPSD

Two ports at the northern entrance of the Suez Canal.

East Port Said
Transshipment hub, with a container terminal run by AP Moller-Maersk
West Port Said
Multi-purpose: containers, general cargo and RoRo, up to 900,000 TEU a year
Serves
The Suez Canal QIZ zone and Port Said governorate
Main lanes
Europe · the Gulf and Asia through the Suez Canal

SOK

Sokhna

Red Sea · Gulf of Suez · EGSOK

Egypt’s main gateway to the Gulf, East Africa and Asia, and the closest of the five to Greater Cairo.

Terminal
DP World runs basins 1–2; terminals CT1 and CT2 can handle 1.1 and 1.8 million TEU
Serves
10th of Ramadan, Obour, Badr and Greater Cairo
Main lanes
The Gulf, East Africa and Asia via the Red Sea, without a canal transit
